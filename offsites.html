Class IV Leadership: In the Field

Class IV in the Field

Some conversations need a different room.

Immersive and place-based leadership experiences where teams can see and practice how they operate under consequential conditions.

Hawai‘i  ·  Colorado, Green & Salmon river trips  ·  Custom Class IV field programs

The Purpose

Not adventure for adventure's sake.

The setting matters because it changes the conversation. In the right environment, patterns become visible faster: who leads, who hesitates, how decisions get made, where trust holds, and where communication breaks down.

Ascentric uses the field as a mirror for the same work at the center of the core practice: trust, communication, decision-making, group dynamics, and leadership under pressure. The adventure is not the point. The adventure creates the conditions for honest conversation, stronger trust, and clearer ways of moving together.

This is Class IV Leadership, practiced somewhere the conditions can’t be faked.

Formats

Possible formats.

Hawai‘i

Leadership Immersions

Field-based experiences for founders, executives, and leadership teams that need spaciousness, reflection, trust-building, and strategic reset. Designed for depth, not distraction.

Colorado · Green · Salmon

River-Based Leadership Trips

Multi-day facilitated river experiences designed around trust, communication, adaptability, and decision-making under pressure. The river creates real-time conditions; the facilitation makes them useful.

Custom

Custom Field Programs

Focused experiences for founders, executives, and leadership teams that need space away from normal operating rhythms to work on something meaningful together. These may take place in Boulder or elsewhere depending on the group, purpose, and setting.

The Work

What teams work on.

The setting changes. The work is the same: helping teams see how they actually operate, name what's been invisible, and build stronger ways of moving together.

  • —Trust under pressure
  • —Communication norms
  • —Decision-making and role clarity
  • —Repair and honest feedback
  • —Founder-to-team transition
  • —Shared agreements and next steps
  • —Culture that moves from stated values to lived behavior

Who This Is For

Best for.

  • —Founder teams navigating growth or transition
  • —Executive teams that need to reset trust and communication
  • —VC or accelerator cohorts
  • —Leadership teams preparing for a new stage
  • —Executive groups ready for deep, substantive work
  • —Teams that need to step outside the ordinary room to have a different conversation

How It Works

Designed responsibly.

Every experience is custom scoped based on team size, location, risk profile, facilitation needs, and desired outcomes. For outdoor or river-based programs, Ascentric partners with experienced local operators and designs the facilitation around the team's real objectives.
