01 / Strategy & Organization

Make the next chapter
work in practice.

Strategy becomes useful when people know what matters, how decisions get made, and what they are responsible for.

Ascentric helps leaders work through consequential questions, synthesize competing perspectives, and turn them into a clear organizational direction.

Questions worth working on

  • What does our next stage require of the organization?
  • Where should decisions live, and who needs to be involved?
  • How do we turn competing priorities into a usable plan?

Where we help

Strategic planning · Organizational design · Leadership structure · Decision rights · Strategic initiatives · Facilitation and synthesis

Depending on scope: strategic priorities, organizational recommendations, decision agreements, or a practical plan for a critical initiative.

02 / Culture & Teams

Change how the
work happens.

The strategy may be clear. The way people work together may still belong to an earlier chapter.

We make those patterns visible and help teams build the trust, candor, role clarity, and shared practices their work now requires.

Explore Culture Cartography™ →

Questions worth working on

  • What are we working around instead of working through?
  • What gets rewarded, avoided, or left unsaid?
  • What would allow this leadership team to act as a team?

Where we help

Culture transformation · Culture by design · Leadership-team effectiveness · Founder / cofounder alignment · Team facilitation · Operating norms

Depending on scope: a culture map, leadership priorities, team agreements, and a practice plan that connects insight to daily work.

03 / Executive Leadership

Grow into what
the role requires.

A thought partner for the decisions, relationships, and responsibilities that come with leading.

Executive coaching connects individual development to the organization around you: the habits you reinforce, the conversations you avoid, and the conditions you create for others.

Where we help

  • Founder and CEO development
  • Leadership transitions and increased responsibility
  • Judgment, communication, and emotional intelligence
  • Leadership-team development
