Path 3

Culture Cartography™

A diagnostic and team alignment process for leadership teams navigating trust, communication, decision-making, and culture under pressure.

03

Culture Cartography

Maps what's actually happening beneath the surface — in trust, communication, decision-making, roles, and group dynamics. The output is a clear diagnostic read and a concrete strategy for what to do with it.

Not a values exercise. Not a retreat. A real diagnostic process that gives leadership teams shared language and a map to navigate deliberately.

4-Step Process · Founder teams, executive teams, and leadership teams

Works Well For Leadership teams with recurring trust or communication breakdowns. Co-founders navigating dynamic or role friction. Teams growing faster than their operating norms. Something feels off but hasn't been named clearly.

What You Leave With A named culture map. Shared language. Practical levers — structural, relational, narrative — for the changes that will actually shift things.
