01

Summit Session™

One focused session. You bring the challenge — a decision you can't make, a dynamic you can't name, a strategic moment where you need another perspective. Adam brings structured inquiry, real-time reflection, and the kind of blunt precision most advisors won't give you.

No ongoing commitment required. This is the right starting point if you need clarity fast, want to test the working dynamic, or have a specific problem that needs a third voice now.

One Session · 90 Minutes · Remote or In-Person

Ideal For A high-stakes decision you've been sitting on. A co-founder dynamic that needs a third voice. Before a board meeting, a difficult conversation, or a major pivot. Founders or executives wanting to test the working dynamic before committing.

What You Leave With A named diagnosis of the situation. Clear next steps. Pressure-tested thinking. A perspective you couldn't generate alone.

Climb with Clarity™

4–6 Bi-Weekly Sessions · 60–75 Minutes Each

Hybrid Support

Summit Series™

For leaders who need more than coaching but less than a full consulting engagement. Summit Series combines strategic coaching, decision support, and practical leadership architecture — while you're in the middle of a live growth or transition moment.

What It Is

Not generic coaching. Not a full consulting engagement. A time-bound hybrid engagement that combines strategic coaching, practical frameworks, and hands-on support — focused on a specific transition, team challenge, or growth moment.

Typical Use Cases

  • — Founder actively building the next layer of leadership
  • — Executive structuring team rhythm and operating norms
  • — Co-founders working through dynamic, role, or communication friction
  • — Scaling leader who needs decision support and practical architecture, not just reflection

What's Included

  • — Weekly or bi-weekly coaching sessions (60–75 min)
  • — Async support between sessions (voice, text, or written)
  • — Practical frameworks and operating tools tailored to the situation
  • — Team observation or facilitation if needed
  • — Named deliverables: operating agreements, leadership architecture, clarity maps

Team & Culture Work

For leadership teams
and co-founders under strain.

When the work needs to happen at the team level — trust, alignment, operating norms, communication, culture architecture — these are the engagements designed for it.

04

Basecamp Intensive

A facilitated half-day or full-day team session for leadership teams navigating major decisions, co-founder dynamics, a culture reset, or the friction of rapid growth.

Not a team-building exercise. Not a trust fall. A structured working session that surfaces what's actually happening, names it clearly, and produces real agreements — not just better feelings.

Half-Day or Full-Day · On-Site or Remote · Teams of 4–20

Ideal For Series A/B leadership teams. Pre-fundraise alignment sessions. Post-merger culture integration. Co-founder relationships under strain. Teams growing faster than their operating agreements.

What You Leave With A clear culture map. Named agreements. An operating framework the team has authored — not inherited.

05

Alignment Arc™

A multi-session team engagement for leadership teams that need sustained work — not a one-day fix. The Alignment Arc is structured over 6–10 sessions and integrates coaching, facilitation, and Culture Cartography™ diagnostics to move the team from misalignment to operating coherence.

Works across roles, communication styles, and trust gaps to produce lasting changes in how the team makes decisions, resolves friction, and builds shared momentum.

6–10 Sessions · 3–6 Month Engagement

Ideal For Leadership teams with recurring trust or communication breakdowns. Co-founders who need structural support to the working relationship. Executive teams scaling faster than their operating norms were built to handle.
