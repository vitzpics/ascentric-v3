How We See the System

Culture Cartography™

A structured process for leadership teams that can feel the friction but have not yet named the pattern.

03

Culture Cartography™

Culture Cartography maps how trust, communication, decisions, roles, and norms are actually operating — drawing on interviews, observation, and validated assessments selected for the question. The tools change. The purpose stays the same: make the invisible dynamics visible so the team knows what actually needs to change. Not a values exercise. A real diagnostic that gives leadership teams shared language and a map to navigate deliberately.

Structured diagnostic process · Leadership teams · Founder teams

Works Well For

  • Recurring trust or communication breakdowns
  • Co-founder or leadership-team friction
  • Teams growing faster than their working norms
  • A persistent sense that something is off but remains unnamed

You Leave With

  • A shared map of the patterns shaping the team
  • Clearer language for what has been difficult to discuss
  • Practical priorities for changing how the team works
