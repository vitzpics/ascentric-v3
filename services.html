Pathway 1

Founder Coaching

For founders navigating pressure, role transition, decision fatigue, early culture design, or the shift from doing everything to leading through others. Ascentric works with founders early — before a full team exists — and alongside founders building the leadership layer.

01

Fit Session™ — Quick Entry

A 90-minute strategic intensive for founders who need clarity under pressure. You bring the problem — a decision you can't make, a dynamic you can't name, a moment where you need another perspective. Adam brings structured inquiry, real-time reflection, and the kind of honest precision most advisors won't give you.

No ongoing commitment required. The right starting point if you need clarity fast, want to test the working dynamic, or have a specific problem that needs a third voice now.

One Session · 90 Minutes · Remote or In-Person

Ideal For A high-stakes decision you've been sitting on. A co-founder dynamic that needs a third voice. Before a board meeting, a difficult conversation, or a major pivot. Founders wanting to test the working dynamic before committing.

What You Leave With A named diagnosis of the situation. Clear next steps. Pressure-tested thinking. A perspective you couldn't generate alone.

02

First Ascent — Coaching Series

A structured coaching series designed to establish your base, sharpen your focus, and step into your next chapter with clarity and intention. Built for founders ready to move with more precision — not more effort.

Sessions combine strategic inquiry, honest reflection, and practical integration work designed to close the gap between who you are and who your role demands you become.

4–6 Bi-Weekly Sessions · 60–75 Minutes Each

Ideal For Founders navigating the shift from doing to leading. Early-stage CEOs building culture before the team exists. Leaders who are performing but feel friction they can't name.

What Moves Decision clarity. Leadership presence. Team trust. The gap between your vision and your team's reality.

Pathway 2

Culture Drag Snapshot /
Culture Cartography™

A focused diagnostic for teams where growth is creating friction in communication, decision-making, role clarity, trust, or shared ownership. Maps what's actually happening beneath the surface — then builds a practical path forward.

Culture Drag Snapshot

A focused, time-limited diagnostic for teams that need a fast read on what's creating friction. Discovery interviews, synthesis, and a clear debrief. Produces a practical map of where the drag is and what to do about it.

Full Culture Cartography™

A comprehensive diagnostic engagement that maps culture across all three dimensions: surface (what we say), behavioral (how we act), and identity (who we're becoming). Produces a named map plus a strategic architecture for evolution.

Works Well For

  • — Leadership teams with recurring alignment problems
  • — Cofounders navigating dynamic or role friction
  • — Teams growing faster than their operating norms
  • — Something feels off but hasn't been named clearly

Pathway 3

Team Alignment

Facilitation and leadership team work that turns tension into clearer norms, stronger agreements, and better operating rhythms. For teams that need work done at the team level — not just individual coaching.

04

Basecamp Intensive

A facilitated half-day or full-day team session for leadership teams navigating major decisions, co-founder dynamics, a culture reset, or the friction of rapid growth.

Not a team-building exercise. Not a trust fall. A structured working session that surfaces what's actually happening, names it clearly, and produces real agreements — not just better feelings.

Half-Day or Full-Day · On-Site or Remote · Teams of 4–20

Ideal For Founding teams before or after a fundraise. Post-hire alignment moments. Co-founder relationships under strain. Teams growing faster than their operating agreements. Leadership teams with pre-existing friction that needs to surface.

What You Leave With A clear culture map. Named agreements. An operating framework the team has authored — not inherited.

Inquire About a Basecamp
05

Alignment Arc™

A multi-session team engagement for leadership teams that need sustained work — not a one-day fix. The Alignment Arc is structured over 6–10 sessions and integrates coaching, facilitation, and Culture Cartography™ diagnostics to move the team from misalignment to operating coherence.

Works across roles, communication styles, and trust gaps to produce lasting changes in how the team makes decisions, resolves friction, and builds shared momentum.

6–10 Sessions · 3–6 Month Engagement

Ideal For Leadership teams with recurring trust or communication breakdowns. Co-founders who need structural support to the working relationship. Executive teams scaling faster than their operating norms were built to handle.
