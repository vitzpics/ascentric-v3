Path 3 — Team Diagnostic

Culture Cartography™

A diagnostic and team alignment process for leadership teams navigating trust, communication, decision-making, and culture under pressure. Maps what's actually happening — then builds a strategy for what to do with it.

04

Culture Cartography™ Engagement

Most culture problems can be felt long before they can be named. Culture Cartography gives teams a structured way to see what's happening beneath the surface — and shared language to work with it deliberately.

Discovery interviews, culture map synthesis, leadership debrief, and a strategic architecture for the moves that will actually shift things.

4-Step Process · Leadership Teams of 4–20

Works Well For Leadership teams with recurring trust or communication breakdowns. Co-founders navigating dynamic or role friction. Teams growing faster than their operating norms. Something feels off but hasn't been named clearly.

What You Leave With A named culture map. Shared language. A clear strategy for the specific levers — structural, relational, narrative — that will shift things.
