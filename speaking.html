Formats

How the work shows up.

01

Keynote or Panel Talk

Talks designed for founder audiences, leadership summits, and entrepreneurial ecosystems. Topics include: building culture at scale, the leadership gap in scaling startups, and what it means to lead at inflection points. 20–60 minutes.

Inquire
02

Workshop — Half Day

Facilitated working sessions for accelerator cohorts, founder communities, or leadership teams. Highly interactive. Leaves participants with a tangible framework, named insight, or operating agreement — not just inspiration. Up to 30 people.

Inquire

Cohort Program — Multi-Session

A series of facilitated sessions built for accelerator batches or founder cohorts over the course of a program. Covers the leadership arc of early-stage company building: co-founder dynamics, team culture, personal leadership, and inflection navigation.

Sample Topics

What the sessions cover.

Leadership

The Founder-to-Leader Transition

How to grow into the CEO your company needs without losing what made you effective as a founder — and how to start building the layer beneath you.

Culture

Culture From the Beginning

Why the most important time to build culture is before you think you need to — and how to do it without making it a values exercise.

Co-Founders

Cofounder Dynamics

Designing the working relationship before it breaks under pressure. Agreements, communication, and trust at the top — built before you need them.

Communication

Communication Under Pressure

How teams lose the ability to talk honestly when things move fast — and how to build communication norms that hold when it's actually hard.

Decisions

Decision-Making at Scale

Moving from founder-held decisions to team-held decisions — and building the clarity, trust, and operating agreements that make that transition stick.

Group Development

How Groups Actually Work

The real mechanics of how teams form, struggle, and eventually move together — and what founders can do to accelerate the arc rather than manage the friction.
