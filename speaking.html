Speaking & Workshops

The human side of growth,
leadership, and change.

Adam speaks and facilitates for conferences, founder communities, leadership teams, and organizations navigating the parts of growth that rarely show up on a strategy deck.

What Makes This Different

Adam does more than deliver a talk. He knows how to work with what is happening in the room.

That may mean telling a story, naming a pattern the group has been avoiding, shifting into reflection, or turning an idea into a conversation people can actually use. The work is designed to land in the room, not just sound good from the stage.

Who This Is For

For the people bringing leaders together.

Conferences & Summits

Keynotes, panels, and interactive sessions for audiences navigating leadership, growth, culture, and change.

Founder Communities & Accelerators

Talks, workshops, and cohort sessions for founders building companies while also becoming the leaders those companies require.

Executive & Leadership Teams

Focused sessions for teams working through communication strain, trust, decision-making, transition, or group dynamics under pressure.

Sample Topics

What the sessions cover.

Leadership

The Founder-to-Leader Transition

What changes when the person who started the company can no longer hold every decision, relationship, and layer of leadership themselves.

Group Dynamics

How Groups Actually Work

Why capable individuals do not automatically become a capable team, and what leaders can do when trust, communication, and coordination start to break down.

Communication

Communication Under Pressure

What happens to honesty when the stakes rise, and how teams build the ability to have the conversations they keep postponing.

Culture

Culture From the Beginning

Why culture starts long before a company writes down its values, and how early choices become norms that are difficult to unwind later.

Co-Founders

Cofounder Dynamics

How trust, roles, expectations, and unresolved tension at the top shape the rest of the company.

Identity & Leadership

The Lineage We Carry

A workshop exploring how ancestry, identity, place, and responsibility shape the way we lead, belong, and make meaning.
