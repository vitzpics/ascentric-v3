Recognize any of this?

When work gets harder than it should be, look at how you’re working together.

  • Decisions keep getting revisited.
  • Roles and authority become blurry.
  • People stop saying what they really think.
  • Too many decisions land with the same few people.
  • The strategy changes. Behavior doesn’t.
  • Strong people start working around one another.

What worked at one stage stops working at the next.
